# Camera Move & Element Motion Guide (i2v)

## Camera Moves (`camera_move`)

- **`push_in`**: Slow forward zoom. Establishes focus on wide shot `a`.
- **`parallax`**: 2D depth shift. Great for detail shot `b`.
- **`pan`**: Horizontal scan across the collage landscape.
- **`tilt`**: Vertical movement up or down across newspaper headlines.
- **`static`**: Zero camera movement. Use on the final payoff shot for emphasis.

## Element Motion Guidelines

Describe active movement for cut-out paper elements:

- _"paper plane glides across frame from left to right, cut-out figures gesture, coins scatter"_
- _"halftone background pulses gently, headline text remains static while clock gears turn"_
- _"cut-out hands slide together in central exchange, paper sparkles pop"_
