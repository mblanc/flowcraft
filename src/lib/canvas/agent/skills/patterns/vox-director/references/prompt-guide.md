# Vox Style Prompt Guide & Theme Presets

## 5-Part Vox Prompt Formula

1. **Medium**: Modern editorial paper-collage, hand-cut paper cut-outs, torn edges, tape strips, halftone dot overlays, newspaper clippings.
2. **Era / Theme Preset**: Match topic tone with theme preset or Canvas `STYLE.md`.
3. **Color Palette**: Bold flat background color per beat (earthy clay tan, vivid mustard yellow, matte dark navy).
4. **Typography**: Big bold cut-out newspaper headline text in ALL CAPS (wide shots only).
5. **Finish**: Paper grain, printmaker ink texture, flat lighting.

## Theme Presets

- **`american-retro`**: 1950s WPA graphic art propaganda, faded crimson, ochre, matte black ink cut-outs, distressed newsprint background.
- **`swiss-modern`**: International typographic style, grid alignment, vivid vermilion and royal blue color blocks, clean geometric paper shapes.
- **`punk-zine`**: High contrast black and white photocopy texture, neon yellow accent tape, torn magazine edges, raw cut-outs.
- **`soviet-constructivist`**: Diagonal dynamic composition, stark red, black, and cream flat blocks, bold angular cut-out typography.
- **`70s-groovy`**: Warm burnt orange, mustard yellow, avocado green, wavy paper cut-out layers, halftone textures.
